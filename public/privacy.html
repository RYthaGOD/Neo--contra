Privacy Policy

NEOCONTRA: SOLANA ASSAULT

Last updated: 23 August 2026

NeoContra: Solana Assault is a browser-based game, also distributed as an Android app through the Solana dApp Store. This policy describes exactly what the game does and does not collect. It is written to match the code, not to cover hypotheticals.

The short version. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no email, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a leaderboard entry — and only if you press SUBMIT SCORE yourself.

What is stored on your device

Saved in your browser's localStorage, on your device only:

  • neocontra.skin — which player skin you have equipped.
  • neocontra.skins.owned — which cosmetic skins you have unlocked.

This never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. Clearing your browser or app storage erases it, which also clears your unlocked cosmetics.

What is sent to us

Exactly one thing, and only when you choose it. If you have a wallet connected and press SUBMIT SCORE on the game-over screen, the game sends:

  • your wallet's public address;
  • your score;
  • the level you reached.

We store those three values with a timestamp so the global leaderboard can be ranked and displayed. A public wallet address is by design public information and is shown on the leaderboard. If you never press SUBMIT SCORE, nothing is ever sent.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any device identifier. We do not use cookies for tracking.

Payments

Purchases in the Armory, cosmetic skins and pay-to-continue revives are plain transfers of SOL, signed by your own wallet and sent directly to the game's treasury address on the Solana blockchain.

We never take custody of your funds, and we never see, request, store or transmit your private keys or seed phrase. Every transaction is approved by you inside your own wallet app. Solana transactions are public and permanent by the nature of a public blockchain.

Third parties

Using the game causes requests to these services, which will see your IP address:

  • Our host (Railway). Serves the game and the leaderboard API, and keeps standard server logs.
  • A Solana RPC provider. Used to read balances and broadcast your transactions to the network. It receives your wallet address and IP.
  • Google Fonts. Serves the retro typeface used by the interface.
  • Your wallet app (for example Phantom or Solflare). A separate application governed by its own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and there is no advertising in the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. It involves cartoon pixel-art violence and real-value cryptocurrency purchases, and is intended for an adult audience.

Your choices and deletion

  • Don't submit. Simply never press SUBMIT SCORE and nothing is sent.
  • Play without a wallet. The game is fully playable unconnected; a wallet is only needed for purchases and the leaderboard.
  • Local data. Clear your browser or app storage to erase everything held on your device.
  • Leaderboard removal. Contact us with your wallet address and we will delete that entry.

On-chain transactions cannot be deleted by us or by anyone — that is a property of the Solana blockchain, not a choice this game makes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the game's release notes.
